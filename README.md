## Hi there 👋. I'm Nicolás, a mathematician who turns messy, real-world problems into models, code and clear decisions.

For 8+ years I've taught calculus, linear algebra, differential equations and programming at universities in Colombia and Chile — currently at Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez and Universidad Finis Terrae. Explaining complex results to people who don't speak math is what I do every day, and it's exactly what data teams need.

What I bring:
• Quantitative depth: M.Sc. in Mathematics (UFU, Brazil; research stay at IMPA) and doctoral coursework with qualifying exams passed (PUC Chile, Universidad de Chile; ANID National Doctoral Fellowship 2021).
• Data work: Python (pandas, NumPy, TensorFlow) and R (tidyverse, Shiny). Analysis of occupational-health and medical-leave data for a consulting project in the Chilean banking sector; interactive dashboards for freelance clients.
• Algorithms: I coach ICPC-style competitive programming teams.
• Communication: data storytelling, reproducible reports (LaTeX, notebooks), dashboards.

Open to data scientist / data analyst roles — hybrid in Santiago or remote.
